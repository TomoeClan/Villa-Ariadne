1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how Villa Ariadne ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects the personal data of visitors to this website and prospective guests who submit enquiries.

2. What data we collect

When you submit an enquiry through our website, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your intended arrival and departure dates
  • Number of guests
  • Any additional information you include in your message

We also collect technical data automatically when you visit the website, including your IP address and browser type, through standard server logs and embedded third-party content (see Section 5).

When you visit our website, a Google Ads tag may collect advertising and behavioural data, including your IP address and browsing behaviour, to measure the effectiveness of our campaigns and enable remarketing.

3. Why we collect it and the legal basis

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your enquiry and, if applicable, confirm a booking - legal basis: performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)
  • To manage our ongoing relationship with guests - legal basis: legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)
  • To comply with legal obligations, such as tax or accounting requirements - legal basis: legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR)
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns and retarget previous visitors through Google Ads - legal basis: consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)

4. How long we keep your data

If your enquiry does not result in a booking, we retain your data for up to 12 months, after which it is deleted.

If a booking is made, we retain relevant records for up to 7 years to comply with Greek tax and accounting obligations.

Google Ads data is retained according to Google's own retention policies, which you can review at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Third-party services

Our website embeds a Google Maps map. When you load the page, Google may collect data about your visit, including your IP address. This is subject to Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

We use a Google Ads tag to measure the effectiveness of our paid advertising campaigns and to show our ads to people who have previously visited our site (remarketing). Google may collect data including your IP address, browser type, and browsing behaviour for this purpose. You can opt out of personalised advertising at https://adssettings.google.com.

We do not display third-party advertisements on this website and we do not sell your data to any third party.

6. Data transfers outside the EU

Google Maps and Google Ads are operated by Google LLC, based in the United States. Google relies on Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission as the legal basis for transferring data outside the EU.

7. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where consent is the legal basis for processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

For Greece, the supervisory authority is the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (HDPA): www.dpa.gr

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us using the enquiry form on our website. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Contact

You can reach us using the enquiry form at villaariadneportoheli.com

Cookie Notice

Cookies on this website

This website uses two types of cookies: necessary cookies that allow the site to function, and optional advertising cookies that require your consent.

Necessary cookies

These are required for the website to work and cannot be switched off. They include cookies set by Google Maps, which is embedded on this page to show the villa's location.

Advertising cookies (optional - consent required)

We use a Google Ads tag to track visits from our advertising campaigns and to show Villa Ariadne ads to previous visitors of this site. If you accept advertising cookies, Google may collect data including your IP address and browsing behaviour for this purpose. These cookies are only activated with your consent.

You can withdraw your consent or opt out of personalised advertising at any time at https://adssettings.google.com.

How to manage cookies

You can change your preferences at any time through your browser settings. Withdrawing consent will not affect the lawfulness of any processing carried out before you withdrew it.
